Free and paid

Wishzine is free to download. The free part includes one list and two styles, and what you send carries a small "Made with Wishzine" line. Wishzine Plus is a one-time purchase that unlocks every style, including ones added later, as many lists as you like and sends without that line. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Use Restore in the app to get purchases back on a new iPhone.
