Support

Write to eprivalovdev@gmail.com. I read every message and usually answer within two days.

A link didn't work

Some stores don't show their pages to apps. Wishzine then offers to open the page inside the app; once it has loaded, add the gift from there. You can also add the gift with your own photo and paste the link by hand.

The picture isn't cut out

When a photo has no clear single subject (a room, a flat lay), Wishzine keeps it as a photo card. Replace it with your own photo from the gift's menu if you like.

Friends can't open a gift

Links work in the PDF. Most messengers and mail apps open them with a tap; if one shows only a preview, open the PDF itself and tap again. The Story image is a picture, so it has no links.

Restore a purchase

On a new iPhone, open the paywall or the menu on your shelf and tap Restore. Purchases come back with the same Apple Account.

Moving to a new iPhone

Lists are stored on the iPhone and travel with an iPhone backup or a direct transfer. There is no account to sign into.
