Privacy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Wishzine collects no data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer runs no server for the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Your lists, the gifts on them, notes, drawings and pictures are saved inside the app on your iPhone. Like other app data they can be part of your iPhone or iCloud backup, which Apple handles. The developer never receives them.

Store pages

When you add a link, your iPhone downloads that page and its picture straight from the store, the same way Safari would. The store sees an ordinary request from your device and its own privacy policy applies. Pages that a store shows only in a browser open inside Wishzine in a standard web view.

Photos

When you add your own photo, you pick it in the system photo picker. Wishzine gets only the photo you choose and keeps a copy with your list.

Reminders

If you turn on a reminder for a list, it is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The share button

Sharing a link to Wishzine from another app passes it to Wishzine on the same iPhone. The PDF or image you send goes only to the people you choose, through the app you choose.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple. Wishzine learns only which purchase is active on your Apple Account, never your payment details.

Children

Wishzine collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
